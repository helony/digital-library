Leyl û Mecnûn

Şêx Mihemed Can · Kurmancî

A 352-page Kurmanji verse telling of Leyl and Mecnûn, by the classical Kurdish poet Şêx Mihemed Can. This 1992 edition presents the work in Latin and Kurdish Arabic scripts.
