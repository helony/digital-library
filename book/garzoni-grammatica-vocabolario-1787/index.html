Grammatica e vocabolario della lingua Kurda

Maurizio Garzoni · Kurmancî / Bahdînî

Maurizio Garzoni’s 1787 grammar and vocabulary, based on Kurdish learned in the Bahdinan region. It is one of the earliest substantial printed studies of Kurdish.
