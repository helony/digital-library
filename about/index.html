ABOUT THE LIBRARY

Kurdish books, open to everyone

The Kurdish Digital Library brings together books and historical texts from across Kurdish varieties. You can browse the whole catalogue in any interface language, read available texts, and follow each record to its original source.

What you can find here

The catalogue includes poetry, stories, historical works, and language resources in Kurmancî, Soranî, Zazakî, Hewramî/Goranî, and Southern Kurdish. Each record lists its author, source, format, availability, and rights information. Some works are partial, and those records say so.

Preserving the collection

Every book has a permanent record. The preservation dashboard shows which eligible works have a copy hosted by this library and which still depend on an external source. The original source stays linked for attribution and provenance.

Rights and reuse

Being readable online does not always mean a scan, translation, or transcription can be redistributed. The library only automates local copies where reuse evidence is sufficiently clear. Other records remain available to browse while their rights need review. See the rights and reuse policy for details.
