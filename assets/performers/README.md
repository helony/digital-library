# Performer illustrations and source notes

`voice-sun.svg`, `voice-record.svg` and `voice-weave.svg` are original vector illustrations created for Kurdish Digital Library on 2026-09-25. They depict a voice, recorded sound and woven ornament. They are symbolic illustrations, not likenesses of any performer or reconstructions of historical portraits. No external photograph or illustration was traced or incorporated.

Credit: **Illustration · Kurdish Digital Library**. The illustrations are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The real video stills for Mohamad Saeed, Golala and Zakia are the existing CC BY-SA 4.0 assets in `assets/previews/`; their original credits and source pages remain attached to each profile in `data/recordings.json`. See `../previews/README.md` for provenance. The musical recordings remain with their publishers and are not copied or presented as openly licensed.

## Profile introductions

Short introductions describe documented biographical facts or the recordings actually present in this collection. Dates and unsupported biographical details were deliberately omitted where reliable source material was unavailable. The source URLs are stored on each performer as `sources` for display and future maintenance.

- Şakiro: the Şakiro Foundation's own Kurdish biography, https://dengbejsakirovakfi.org/ku/dengbej-sakiro/biyografi (accessed 2026-09-25).
- Ayşe Şan and Îsa Berwarî: Kurdish Institute of Paris, December 1996 bulletin, https://www.institutkurde.org/publications/bulletins/pdf/141.pdf; collection recording Qederê published by SesMedia.
- Dengbêj Gazîn: Orient-Institut Istanbul / Max Weber Stiftung scholarly edition, https://qed.perspectivia.net/gazin (accessed 2026-09-25).
- The other musical profiles describe the credited recordings linked in their `sources`; they do not infer dates, places, nationality or life histories.

The Zembîlfiroş connection identifies the written text, library retelling and Karapetê Xaço recording as different tellings of a shared story. It does not assert that the performance is a word-for-word audiobook or that disputed traditional authorship is settled.

`hac` and `sdh` currently share the `ckb` interface translation, identified in `localeFallbacks`; these strings must not be represented as native Hawrami or Southern Kurdish translations.
