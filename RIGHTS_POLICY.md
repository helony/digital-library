# Rights & Reuse Policy

The Kurdish Digital Library separates three questions:

1. Is the underlying historical work out of copyright?
2. Is the specific edition, transcription, translation or scan reusable?
3. May the library redistribute its own local copy in the jurisdiction where it is hosted?

Records marked archive-eligible have sufficiently clear evidence for the library's automated preservation workflow. Records marked regional, review, or sharing-permitted are not automatically mirrored.

Original source URLs remain part of the permanent metadata even when a local copy exists. They are provenance, attribution and evidence, not the sole dependency of the library.

If a rights concern is reported, the preferred response is to disable distribution of the disputed local file while preserving the bibliographic record and review history.
